# Transcribe Audio to Text — AI-Powered Audio & Video Transcription
 
Turning recordings into accurate, searchable text shouldn't take hours of manual work. **[Transcribe Audio to Text](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/transcribe-audio-to-text/gfhhgpmndeebbmpojhjpgdpbbapffaka)** is a Chrome extension that uses AI to convert audio and video files into clean transcripts in minutes — right from your browser.
 
Whether it's an interview, a meeting, a podcast, or a voice memo, our tool handles the heavy lifting so you can focus on the content, not the process.
 
---
 
## How It Works
 
1. **Install** the Chrome extension from the Web Store.
2. **Upload or select** your audio or video file.
3. **Start transcription** with a single click.
4. **Let the AI** convert speech to text.
5. **Review** the generated transcript.
6. **Copy or download** it for later use.
---
 
## Key Features
 
- 🎙️ **AI-powered transcription** for fast, accurate results
